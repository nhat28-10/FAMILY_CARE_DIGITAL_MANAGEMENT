# Subscription Pricing Plan

Tai lieu nay la ban mo ta san pham de FE/Admin cau hinh va hien thi pricing.
Khong dung nhu tai lieu API hoac schema backend.

## FREE

Free la goi dung thu cho gia dinh moi. Muc tieu la giup nguoi dung hieu app co
nhung module chinh nao, nhung khong du de su dung lau dai nhu goi tra phi.

### Duoc dung

- Tao gia dinh
- Moi toi da 4 thanh vien
- Xem danh sach thanh vien
- Lich/su kien co ban voi gioi han dung thu
- Cong viec gia dinh co ban voi gioi han dung thu
- Quan ly thu/chi co ban voi gioi han dung thu
- Album gia dinh dung luong thap, khoang 500 MB
- Upload anh
- Chat text co ban trong gia dinh
- Nhan notification co ban
- SOS thu cong
- Gui vi tri hien tai khi bam SOS

### Khong co

- Lich lap lai
- Cong viec lap lai
- Reminder nang cao
- Bao cao tai chinh
- Canh bao ngan sach
- Saving Goal nang cao
- AI phan tich chi tieu
- AI goi y tiet kiem
- AI Assistant trong ban chinh thuc
- Upload video
- Nhan dien/goi y khuon mat
- Real-time location tracking
- Location history
- Garmin/Wearable
- Fall Detection
- Automatic SOS
- Gui file/video trong chat
- Chat nang cao

## MONTHLY

Monthly la goi su dung that cho gia dinh, gia 100,000 VND/thang.

- Khong gioi han hoac gioi han rat cao cho lich, task, giao dich
- Lich lap lai va reminder nang cao
- Cong viec lap lai
- Budget, canh bao ngan sach, bao cao tai chinh
- Saving Goal
- AI phan tich chi tieu
- AI goi y tiet kiem
- AI Assistant quota cao
- Album khoang 5 GB
- Upload anh va video
- Nhan dien/goi y khuon mat
- Chat day du, gui file/video
- Notification nang cao
- SOS thu cong va Advanced SOS
- Real-time location tracking
- Location history
- Garmin/Wearable integration
- Fall Detection
- Automatic SOS

## YEARLY

Yearly co toan bo tinh nang giong Monthly. Khac biet duy nhat la thanh toan theo
nam va co gia uu dai hon, vi du 990,000 VND/nam.

## Luu y cho FE

- FREE nen hien la goi dung thu co gioi han, khong phai goi su dung lau dai.
- Cac quota nhu so lich, so cong viec, so giao dich hoac so luot AI chi nen hien
  con so cu the khi he thong da enforce duoc that.
- Khong khoa SOS thu cong, notification SOS, va gui vi tri hien tai khi SOS o
  Free.
- Gia tri tra phi nen tap trung vao AI, bao cao, canh bao, tu dong hoa, wearable,
  fall detection, real-time tracking, video va face recognition.
